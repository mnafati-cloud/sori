# Sori — pinceau blanc v1 (style 2 validé le 17 octobre 2026)

Create ONE square vocabulary illustration for the Korean learning app Sori.
Match ONLY column 02 « Pinceau » of the reference board: expressive warm-white
brush strokes, restrained warm-grey ink washes, visible bristle marks and large
open black areas inside the subject. Solid pure black background. At most one
small vermilion accent (#E4584A), only where natural for the subject.
Not the speckled rubbing print, not layered paper, not a 3D render or photograph.
One isolated, immediately recognizable subject, centered, occupying 65–80% of
the square; generous empty margins. Complete silhouette with no cropped parts.
Readable at 160 pixels and still recognizable at 96 pixels. No decorative frame,
no scene, no unrelated accessories, no people unless the subject requires them.
NO text, letters, Hangul, labels, numbers, signatures, logos, seals or watermark.
The reference is a style guide, never a layout to reproduce: deliver exactly one
object on one square canvas, never a comparison board, contact sheet or grid.

La référence contient plusieurs styles pour comparaison : seul le deuxième
est autorisé. Les mots et traductions sont rendus par l'application après réponse,
jamais dessinés dans l'image. Les couleurs et concepts abstraits ne deviennent
pas des questions visuelles par simple illustration décorative.
